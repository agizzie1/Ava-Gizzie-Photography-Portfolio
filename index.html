Stockholm on ice

Stockholm: January and February in The Water City.

These photos are a selection of the many shots taken over January and February of 2026 of my time in Stockholm. When I arrived in Stockholm in mid-January of this year, the first thing I noticed was the water, or, rather, the ice. Even as the early morning sky took its time fading from inky black to grey, obscuring most of my surroundings, the icy landscape stood out. As I spent more time in the city, I wanted to document as much of the blend between nature and metropolis as I could, so I set out with my camera.

Many people remarked to me during my initial weeks in the city how unprecedented the days of sunlight we had were, which drove me to photograph more of the city while the weather lasted. Although these photos may not be representative of typical Stockholm winter weather, they reflect the brightness that I felt while taking in the light and trying to capture that feeling in a set of images.
